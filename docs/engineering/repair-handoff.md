# Verified business handoff

The independent host prepares a successor, reserves its native task identity and
stages its business checkpoint after repair verification. Business staging writes
a new draft and its owner's pointers atomically while retaining the intervention
hold and native reservation. A separate native publication then commits the fixed
task and fence release together. Only that authenticated task can activate the
ready business checkpoint; the Case enters observation rather than closure.

## Verified management context

Management schema 16 retains preparation, reservation, application, publication
and business-progress ledgers and the separate `admin_recovery_watch` observation
interval. Schema 16 preserves retained execution provenance and the separate queued-task
hold identity in takeover inspection, including after management restart. The host reads `handoff-pending` only
under current running intent and obtains `handoff-context` with management
authority. Agent commands expose neither action. A display status alone is
insufficient: context recompiles the entire ordered native receipt, compares its
saved bytes, checks the recorded worker identity, physically settled repair/Test
preparation/native execution, exact original coverage, immutable candidate and
owned source/cohort. Changed originals or stopped intent refuse preparation.

The target pins Case and verification identity, original repair and Test
preparation, receipt/input/plan/candidate hashes, source observation and receipt,
repository/resource binding and exact expected content version. Its identity is
independent of the current supervisor so a successor host can retry the same
target, but every operation requires fresh supervision and running intent. The
saved source generation and current management authorization remain distinct.

## Private native preparation

The independent host uses the API-owned private control socket's
`prepare-handoff` action. The API reads the verified context itself; its caller
cannot provide a directory, passing verdict, successor Agent or fence release.
The API revalidates the native source and exact owned fence, reconciles the same
physical writer inventory, and calls the business worker's host-only
`repair-handoff-prepare`. Preparation rechecks the actual repository version and
current source. Readonly roles and worktree execution also require a clean
registered repository. In-place Direct and development can retain uncommitted
work; the exact content version still has to match and preparation discards none
of those changes.

Business SQLite's `engineering_repair_handoff` is the first durable handoff
intent. It retains the
exact old root payload/hash, target, frozen task Team snapshot, original human,
and a deterministic new native task ID. YAML preparation stores a reconciled
successor draft with a new ID, repair-derived binding and incremented sequence.
All thirteen native definitions restart their current stage at its first phase,
without inheriting completed phases, sealed blocks or derived conclusions.
Verification retains source/scenario definitions, but removes results and evidence
review. Development removes implementation claims, captured-check selections and
code-review fingerprints. Other stage artifacts and reproduction observations are
rebuilt from the frozen contract. The surrendered draft retains the old facts
and events. The successor preserves metadata, acceptance definitions,
authenticated human decisions and their exact answered question snapshots;
non-human resolutions reopen for assessment and historical questions become
inactive. It does not create a row in `chain_drafts`. Direct preparation records
its next version without editing its attempts or result. Each native workflow
owner still validates its exact original editable checkpoint.

The API rechecks frozen Team/native origin, human membership/project access,
Agent permissions/resources, actual content, management intent and native fence
after asynchronous preparation. Only then does it acknowledge the exact proposal
hash in management storage. A crash between the two store writes replays the
same proposal and successor identity. Acknowledgement cannot replace a previously
recorded proposal. The Case stays `verified`, its original intervention remains
pending and the fence remains held. A recorded preparation is historical evidence,
not a live execution permission.

## Durable native reservation

Independent discovery now follows preparation with the private `reserve-handoff`
action. Reservation requires the acknowledged preparation and independently
repeats source, physical cohort, frozen Team, human, role-resource and actual
content checks. It compares the complete prepared successor with the saved
acknowledgement; a new passing flag or a task ID from the caller cannot reserve
work.

PostgreSQL migration 667 adds its own `engineering_repair_handoff` table. Under
the project admission lock and exact owned-fence lock, the API checks the writer
inventory, absence of active tasks, fresh management intent and content, and
absence of the proposed successor in the native task queue. It persists the
target, project, stable task ID and full immutable reservation receipt with
`phase='reserved'`. A project can have one outstanding reservation; another
target, changed payload or preexisting successor fails without replacement.
This record creates no `agent_task_queue` row.

The receipt pins proposal/proof hashes, candidate content version, continuation
identity/checkpoint, original source and the precise fence generation/cohort.
Native and kernel implementations hash length-prefixed UTF-8 fields so Unicode
paths and JSON escaping cannot change receipt identity. Management rechecks the
receipt against the current verified context before acknowledging it in schema
10. A crash after the PostgreSQL commit replays the same reservation after fresh
authorization, including under a successor supervisor or a new running-intent
revision. The original fence identity remains historical; current authorization
is checked separately. Stopped intent or new original observations refuse reuse.

Reserved records participate in the database admission and project/resource
mutation guards. Even if a fence is absent, an outstanding reservation prevents
controller/task admission and resource changes until publication commits.
The normal reservation path retains its exact owned fence and business hold.

## Successor evidence and retired authority

Preparation parses original and derived contracts through each native owner's
schema and requires the original draft/contract binding to agree. Review and
Feedback Verify refuse a changed accepted-work commit: a repair receipt cannot
relabel old independent evidence as verification of new delivered work. Normal
independent verification and evidence reconciliation remain required in that
case. The unchanged-commit path still rebuilds the stage's observations.

Surrender history independently revokes the exact old task and daemon attempt,
even if an active issue hold is later removed. Old credentials can read their
frozen end-execution receipt and replay the same immutable intervention; they
cannot bind a repository, reserve/bind another process or advance the current
workflow. Physical exit recording remains available. Draft saves also reject the
surrendered draft ID independently of the supplied workspace/issue binding.
These guards remain active during business staging and release no current hold.

## Held business staging

Independent discovery follows reservation with private `stage-handoff`. The API
reads the management context and exact PostgreSQL reservation itself, validates
the stable task is still absent, checks the original native source/fence/cohort,
physical quiescence, actual content, frozen Team and human/role resources, and
passes only that saved reservation to host-only `repair-handoff-stage`.

The business worker requires the exact acknowledged proposal, current task Team
snapshot and original root bytes. One SQLite transaction inserts the new YAML
draft, changes the current owner's pointers, applies any Feedback batch sidecar
with byte comparison, and saves `engineering_repair_handoff_application`.
Direct updates its version/base and clears the active old attempt, preserving
input and attempt history. Business supersedes the unfinished entry and appends
its successor. Engineering and requirement keep their revision and bounded
budgets while advancing a recovery epoch. Context/planning/reproduction preserve
original source obligations; pending planning and the first managed unit use the
repaired base. Recovered verification uses new observations; old delivery runtime
metadata does not certify the repaired version. Review, Feedback and accepted
work histories remain frozen. A changed accepted requirement-work commit keeps
the hold and requires normal independent verification/evidence reconciliation.

The immutable application witness pins before/after root hashes, the successor
draft, exact Feedback sidecars, original execution/physical-binding bytes and
the reserved continuation. Late root writes and runtime binds compare the
recovery epoch or existing monotonic version; old draft writes remain rejected.
A failed root/sidecar write or changed content rolls back the entire business
transaction. Preparation/staging retries reuse the saved proposal and new draft
identity, including after restart.

Host repair-source reads validate the applied witness, both draft generations,
original bindings, current root/sidecars and frozen Team before returning the
original provenance with explicit `checkpoint_state='staged'`, target and
application hashes. This bridge retains the original failure version separately
from current content identity. Removing a binding or changing either generation
refuses the bridge; staging does not silently accept a changed checkpoint.

After the business commit, the API rechecks actual content, exact lineage,
native reservation/source/cohort, physical cleanup and human/Team authority,
then acknowledges the full application receipt in management schema 11. A lost
acknowledgement replays the same applied checkpoint under fresh running intent.
The Case remains `verified`, the original intervention remains pending, and
both reservation and project fence remain held.

## Task-specific readiness and native publication

Independent discovery follows staging with private `publish-handoff`. The API
loads the acknowledged application, rechecks original source/fence/cohort,
physical cleanup, current intent and saved human/Team/resources, and prepares
ordinary TaskService enqueue parameters without creating work. Under the project
admission lock it validates again and obtains host-only `repair-handoff-ready`.
The business worker checks actual content before and after its SQLite transaction,
exact applied root/draft/sidecars and original bindings, and physical exit.

`engineering_repair_dispatch` persists a ready grant for only the frozen native
task ID. The ordinary coordinator, human writes and unrelated tasks remain held.
The host's execution-admission request supplies the authenticated native task ID;
selection may inspect this task's prepared scope before binding. Only its actual
daemon claim can activate the successor at repository bind. The pending-hold view
then excludes this original observation while immutable surrender history remains.
Original task/daemon attempts and draft IDs stay revoked; process reserve/bind
also recheck durable surrender within their own transaction. Exit receipts remain
permitted. A failed native publication leaves readiness held with no new task.

Migration 668 adds PostgreSQL `engineering_repair_publication` and blocks unrelated
queued inserts while a project has a reserved handoff. The publication transaction
marks the exact reservation published, deletes only its matching owned fence,
creates the stable task through ordinary TaskService parameters, and stores the
immutable ready/publication envelope. These become visible together. The native
scheduler is notified only after commit. Task creation or receipt failure rolls
back the fence, reservation and queue changes together. The business hold still
waits for this task's first authenticated native bind.

A lost management acknowledgement replays the native publication ledger first.
It validates the stored task identity and current management authority/intent;
it does not prepare or stage again against an old root that the successor may
already have changed. Replays may notify a queued task, but never recreate a task
or re-enqueue a terminal one. Native and Node hash the same length-prefixed UTF-8
ready/publication fields. Management saves its exact immutable envelope and moves
the Case to `observing`. Restart and supervisor replacement retain that receipt.

## Ordinary result observation and Case closure

Independent discovery polls `handoff-observing` under current management authority
and running intent, then calls private `observe-handoff`. Observation reads the
already acknowledged immutable native publication. It does not repeat staging
against a root that ordinary work has advanced.

The native host requires the actual recovery execution to be terminal
(`completed` or `failed`), with a completed timestamp and exact dispatch claim.
A native failure is retained; it does not invalidate a separately applied business
result, and it is never rewritten into native success. It rechecks issue/workspace/project, publication and reservation
identity, absence of a new project fence/reservation, and the exact claim's
controllers and process-exit receipts through the native cleanup rules. Other
ordinary tasks may already be running; they cannot substitute for this task's
facts and do not prevent its completed result from being observed. Cancelled,
running, unclaimed or physically unfinished tasks remain observing.

Host-only `repair-business-progress` revalidates immutable application lineage,
original drafts/bindings, frozen Team and task-specific readiness. It requires
this grant's authenticated bound claim, its actual repository binding and scoped
physical exit, without an active intervention. Direct needs its explicit,
immutable RUN/SUBMIT result. YAML needs the exact successor draft, frozen contract
and definition, new attempt and sequence, and a terminal ordinary `phase.complete`
event. The corresponding business, requirement, Feedback or engineering owner
must also have applied its normal result. A completed draft before owner
settlement is insufficient. Saved conclusions, independent results, reports and
captured command receipts are recompiled or compared against their owner facts;
ordinary work may already have moved the current role or removed its worktree.
This observes an accepted historical result, not current repository delivery.

A validated human `prepare-run` can continue the same prepared stage after
answers or an explicit restart. The business worker persists a hash-bound
`recovery_execution` hint with the original entry task, application/readiness
hashes, frozen human/role, current draft and checkpoint. The normal native
enqueue stores it atomically in task context, retaining shared attribution,
resource validation and scheduler notification. An authenticated repository bind
loads this context from PostgreSQL; daemon request fields cannot provide it.
Copied entry context requires official native retry ancestry with unchanged
issue/role/runtime/human at every hop. The business owner must still own the
exact prepared draft; a different stage, unanswered waiting state or changed
contract refuses binding. Unlinked native tasks cannot consume that stage.

The first entry task remains the publication anchor. A later execution result
additionally records `entry_task_id` and `execution_hint_hash`, while `task_id`
and `attempt_id` name the actual new native execution. Both native and business
proofs must agree with that task's saved context. Original v1 entry-result
receipts retain their hash; continuation receipts use the v2 hash prefix.
This links normal work without reopening a completed stage or inheriting its
result. Terminal checkpoint tasks are never re-enqueued.

The business worker saves immutable `engineering_repair_business_progress` for
the target. A native reclaim before application keeps the stable task ID but
binds the new actual claim only after the previous claim's physical exit. It
records `engineering_repair_retired_claim` and rejects old commands, delayed
draft writes, runtime binds and process reservations; physical exit recording
remains available. Applied progress cannot be reassigned to another claim.

The native observation envelope binds business result/event hashes to the exact
terminal task, actual claim, publication and controller/process fact hash. Management
checks that envelope against the fully recompiled verified context and all
original observations, then stores `admin_business_progress` while the Case stays
`observing`. The host reads native and business facts again. Only an identical
fresh envelope can call private `handoff-close`; management rechecks current
supervision, running intent, original coverage, physical settlement and exact
saved progress before entering `closed`. Interrupted progress acknowledgement
replays the same receipt. Closure acknowledgement loss retains the committed
closed Case. New original facts requeue the Case without deleting old proof.

These host operations do not advance a workflow phase, create more tasks or
mark an issue Done. Business specification/report reading and all ordinary
independent delivery/Done gates remain required.

## Bound recovery failure and renewed investigation

An observing Case first attempts ordinary-result observation. A separately
applied business result retains that path even when native execution failed.
Only when no applied result is available does private `observe-handoff` read the
host-only `repair-recovery-checkpoint` capability.

That read pins the immutable application/readiness grant and its current bound
native task/claim. It recompiles the persisted current stage through its owner,
without probing the execution directory or impersonating an Agent read. YAML
requires the exact prepared draft/contract/definition, an editable phase and no
unanswered input. Business snapshots retain the current generation and frozen
upstream conclusions/comments. Requirement snapshots retain their complete
native contract. Direct needs its actual bound unfinished attempt; a host can
capture a failure before RUN without manufacturing a RUN receipt. Completed/abandoned stages and human-question waits produce no editable bound
failure checkpoint. Unbound prepared entries use the distinct path below. Root, snapshot and execution/physical binding hashes
make the eventual hold conditional on the same persisted state.

Under the native project admission lock, the API verifies the actual task is
`failed` or `completed` with a completed timestamp, exact claim and frozen
workspace/issue/project/runtime/role/human. A later task needs its exact saved
recovery context. Any nonterminal issue task, including deferred or queued
retries, keeps observation waiting. Cancelled/running work, closed/Done/triaged
issues, new fences/reservations and changed scopes cannot authorize this hold.
Fresh management intent and the immutable publication are rechecked before the
business write. The native fault witness binds the Case/publication, checkpoint,
actual claim/context, completion time and native failure reason.

Host-only `repair-recovery-hold` rechecks that checkpoint in one business SQLite
transaction, inserts a new immutable intervention/outbox observation and blocks
ordinary writes. It preserves the prior repair, draft, evidence and process
facts. The new observation explicitly records host recovery provenance; it does
not assert the Agent viewed its checkpoint, issued a question or executed Direct
RUN. Old credentials and draft writes become permanently surrendered. Physical
exit reporting remains available, and an unknown provider/controller exit stays
unknown. A logical business hold does not acquire a native repair fence, cancel
processes or grant write access; the existing takeover path must still commit
its owned fence before cancellation and establish physical ownership.

The ordinary business observation bridge validates the fault against the native
publication and supplies its original `repairCaseId`. Management reopens that
same Case as queued, preserving prior verification/publication proofs and
requiring original plus new failure coverage. Delivery acknowledgement follows
management persistence. A lost hold or management acknowledgement replays the
same durable outbox. Stop before final authorization refuses a new hold; a stop
after its durable commit retains that observation without granting execution.

A surrendered recovery grant no longer owns the next Direct generation even
though both generations have a null draft ID. New run hints and binds select the
current recovery; old hints remain refused. Another independently verified
repair can prepare a new successor without deleting either original failure.

## Terminal failure before business binding

A published entry can fail after native claim but before its business bind. The
host now reads an unchanged `ready` grant, its exact staged root/draft/sidecars,
original application, frozen owner and retained physical-binding bytes. It
requires no recorded binding for that entry and captures no Agent view, Direct
RUN or synthetic attempt. The checkpoint reports a null bound attempt and pins
its preceding observation/receipt, actual source attempt and bindings hash.

Under the existing native project/task locks, failure observation still requires
the actual terminal `failed`/`completed` task, completed timestamp, native claim,
publication scope, fresh management intent and no queued/deferred retry. The
native version-two fault witness records that real claim and the separate
preparation provenance. A never-claimed queued task cannot satisfy this path.
Unknown controller/provider exit remains unknown; the hold creates no fence and
cancels no work.

The business transaction inserts `engineering_repair_prebind` and a new immutable
intervention/outbox observation together. The pending view excludes only the
preceding logical hold superseded by that ledger; its original receipt, drafts,
bindings and dispatch history remain intact. The failed grant stays `ready` with
no bound attempt, but its published stage and old hints remain permanently
revoked. A failed outbox insert rolls back that ledger and preserves prior held
admission. Existing databases upgrade the pending view through a savepoint.
Lost replies replay the same business observation and reopen the same Admin Case.

Host source reads revalidate unchanged prepared state and the full retained
application lineage. They explicitly return `execution_binding_origin` as
`retained-preparation` and the actual `execution_source_attempt_id` of the retained
anchor. This is not a physical binding for the failed claim. Native inspection
and management schema 15 retain that distinction and reject a changed provenance
before takeover. Repeated unbound failures retain the ultimate actual binding
source while preserving each intervening failure. Registered content reads and
subsequent repair verification/handoff still use their normal authorization and
physical-exit gates; no synthetic execution row is inserted to satisfy them.

Direct retains its unchanged goal/contract and absent active attempt; another
independently verified handoff can prepare a new version. YAML retains the exact
prepared draft and typed owner pointers, including business upstream generations
and requirement/Feedback facts. A new verified successor is required before a
new native bind; ordinary workflow, result and Done/reading rules still apply.
Partially activated binds and changed staged state need separate recovery work.
Never-claimed published entries have the distinct dispatch path below.

## Runnable stall before any native claim

For an unchanged published `ready` entry, the observer can now act on a fresh,
continuous 20-minute runnable interval. It rereads the private management watch,
current prepared checkpoint, frozen Team/human/resources, controller cleanup and
actual queue selection. Existing queued/deferred/unlinked work keeps observation
waiting. Under project admission and native task locks, the entry must still be
queued with neither dispatch nor start timestamp; a concurrent claim refuses
handoff rather than fabricating an execution fault.

The first durable write is a native queue admission reservation in
`agent_task_queue.context.engineering_dispatch_hold`. Its immutable witness pins
the publication, current checkpoint, original preparation/bindings, exact native
task creation/context and due interval/source/intent. Status stays `queued`,
`claimed_at` and `started_at` remain null, and no cancellation or repair fence is
created. Normal candidate queries and the inner/outer SQL claim predicates refuse
that marker. Other queued work can still use available machine capacity.

Only then does the host write a business hold and immutable outbox. A dispatch
observation has `sourceKind: prepared-dispatch` and a separate `dispatchStall`
witness; it is stored by observation ID in `engineering_dispatch_intervention`.
Its `attemptId` identifies the retained real source anchor, not a claim by the
queued task. The latter has its own UUID and explicit null claim/start facts.
The history/pending views preserve all original receipts while superseding the
prior hold atomically with the new outbox. Permanent draft/grant/hint guards also
include this history. No fake Agent view, Direct RUN, claim timestamp, process or
execution binding is inserted to fit an attempt-keyed row.

The ordinary observation bridge validates the native marker/publication/context
and retained anchor, reopens the same Case, then acknowledges the business outbox.
A lost reply at reservation, business hold, observation or acknowledgement reuses
the same identities. A stopped host cannot advance; a committed native reservation
keeps its queue barrier. After a new running intent, fresh authority can complete
that exact durable handoff without manufacturing another 20-minute interval.
Changed checkpoint or source cannot silently remove the barrier.

Source reads explicitly retain the earlier actual execution anchor and expose
`dispatch_hold_hash` and `dispatch_task_id`. Native inspection and management schema
16 keep both identities, refuse changed source/context or a newly claimed task,
and permit the normal later cancellation of that never-started row. Investigation
still needs its separate owned fence and physical-writer reconciliation before
cancellation or repair writes. Independent verification/handoff prepares a new
native task; the held task is never re-enqueued or treated as delivered work.
Normal Done and reading gates remain independent.

## Remaining recovery work

Observation of a normally applied recovery result and its guarded Case closure
are connected. Bound terminal executions without a result now create an
immutable host business hold and reopen their original Case. Readonly runnable-
dispatch sampling and durable eligible-time evidence are connected through the
[Admin observer](admin.md#dispatch-readiness-observation). Automatic stall
takeback for an active bound stage or its queued continuation, partially-bound
preparation failure holds, source/cohort changes and recovery
of completed drafts without owner settlement still require migration. Waiting
alone does not synthesize a successful result, fault or Case closure. Broader revocation,
runtime/service identity, external dependency and shared-path recovery retain
their separate migration requirements. Real-provider and packaged recovery
acceptance remain outstanding.
