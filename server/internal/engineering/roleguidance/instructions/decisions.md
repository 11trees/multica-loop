Establish a complete Design Tree for this role's frozen scope. Challenge silent assumptions, missing actors, triggers, observable outcomes, normal and exceptional paths, state/rule boundaries, permissions, failure/recovery, compatibility, adjacent effects, scope and verification oracles. For delivery analysis also examine public contracts, data meaning/lifecycle, concurrency, repetition, security and consequential engineering boundaries. Importance depends on differing outcomes or obligations, not implementation ease, reversibility or your preference. Do not expand the original goal to create choices.

Proposing and resolving are separate stages. Register stable nodes, dependencies, mutually exclusive outcomes, consequences, recommendations/reasons and suggested authority before resolving. A frontier is every node whose prerequisites are already closed and whose answer needs no guessed pending answer. Process that whole ready frontier as a batch; dependent nodes belong to later rounds. Investigate factual prerequisites yourself; an unavailable fact holds its dependent branches while other ready branches continue.

Only the current resolution packet supplies effective decision authority. Inherit explicit human decisions under the same keys, upstream commitments and project evidence within their actual authority. A recommendation, current implementation, empty tree or easy reversal cannot replace permission. Submit HUMAN questions as the authorized complete batch; fully autonomous authority closes eligible nodes within frozen scope without overriding explicit decisions or inventing facts. Never propagate a policy guessed from another stage or old execution.

After every round of human, Agent or evidence-based answers, re-traverse the original inputs and all applicable role dimensions, prune branches ruled out by answers and recompute the frontier. Answer review asks whether anything remains undecided, not merely whether the latest answer raised a question. Use explicit incremental return commands for missed branches or new facts. Preserve closed answers; do not ask them again. Continue to synthesis/contract only when every applicable branch was visited or explicitly excluded, factual premises have evidence, impacts/conflicts have dispositions, all in-scope decisions are closed under proper authority and the recomputed frontier is empty. Any decision mode must meet this same completeness standard.
